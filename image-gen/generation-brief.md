# Image Generation Brief

Use this brief when creating new generated images from the uploaded reference set. The goal is to create tasteful, realistic, non-explicit images of the same model across varied angles, outfits, activities, moods, and settings.

## Primary Goal

Generate photorealistic lifestyle, portrait, fashion, dance, resort, and private-setting images of the same adult South Asian woman from the references. Preserve her natural face, long dark wavy hair, warm medium-brown skin, compact medium-curvy body structure, and soft innocent expression.

The images should celebrate natural beauty and curves while staying tasteful, consensual, adult, and non-explicit. Do not limit the creative range to the first generated images. Each generation agent can choose its own tasteful direction as long as it follows the do/don't rules.

## Required Identity Cues

Every generation should preserve:

- Adult South Asian woman.
- Warm medium-brown skin tone.
- Soft oval face and calm, innocent expression.
- Long dark wavy hair, often half-tied or clipped.
- Compact medium-curvy body structure.
- Natural waist-to-hip curve, fuller hips and thighs.
- Realistic proportions and grounded posture.
- Subtle jewelry or simple everyday accessories when useful.
- Soft natural body details rather than airbrushed perfection.
- Body shape should remain consistent even when outfit category changes.

## Body Shape Model

Use this as the underlying body model for new outfits and angles:

- Compact, soft, naturally curvy adult frame.
- Short-to-average visual height impression; do not stretch the body into fashion-model proportions.
- Soft shoulders and arms, natural bust, compact torso.
- Gentle waist indentation, not a sharp fitness waist.
- Fuller hips and thighs with realistic roundness and soft transitions.
- Proportionate legs with natural thigh volume.
- Natural posture, with realistic body depth from side view.
- Long wavy hair often changes the apparent upper-body outline; keep it voluminous and real.

Important: infer a consistent body beneath clothing, but do not overstate hidden anatomy. Different fabrics change the apparent outline. Jeans compress, trousers smooth, knits hug, dresses drape, swimwear reveals more contour, and dancewear may emphasize movement.

## Style

- Photorealistic lifestyle photography.
- Natural phone-camera realism with polished lighting.
- Warm, cozy, private, believable interiors.
- Candid editorial feel rather than studio glamour.
- Natural skin texture, natural fabric folds, realistic hair.

Avoid plastic skin, over-slimming, exaggerated curves, glamour retouching, or fantasy styling.

## Mood

The mood should be intimate, cozy, and gently teasing without becoming explicit. Think suggestion through posture, outfit, and silhouette, not exposure.

Good emotional tones:

- Innocent.
- Calm.
- Focused.
- Slightly shy.
- Softly confident.
- Relaxed and private.
- Naturally beautiful.

Avoid aggressive seduction, explicit posing, or exaggerated facial expressions.

## Outfit Direction

Outfits may vary widely. The core rule is not "always covered in the same way"; the core rule is "adult, tasteful, non-explicit, and visually coherent." Shape may be celebrated through silhouette, drape, pose, and fabric.

Good outfit families:

- Mini-length ribbed sweater dress with sleeves.
- Soft knit dress in burgundy, brown, charcoal, cream, or muted green.
- Modest wrap dress.
- Shirt dress with cardigan.
- Fitted top with high-waist jeans.
- Cozy lounge dress that remains fully covered.
- Office-casual outfit adapted for a private setting.
- Tasteful poolside swimwear, styled like lifestyle/resort photography.
- Elegant cover-up, sarong, robe, or oversized shirt layered over swimwear.
- Babydoll-style nightdress or slip-inspired sleepwear, opaque and covered.
- Belly dancer-inspired costume with ornate skirt, jewelry, and movement, avoiding explicit exposure.
- Athens/Mediterranean resort dress, draped fabric, gold jewelry, warm stone backdrop.
- Saree, lehenga, kurti, Indo-western dress, or festive fusion outfit.
- Dancewear, resortwear, sleepwear, casual homewear, officewear, or vacationwear.

Rules:

- Clothing can be short, fitted, swimwear-like, dance-inspired, or sleepwear-inspired if the result remains non-explicit.
- Fashion swimwear is allowed; pornographic framing is not.
- Babydoll/nightdress styling is allowed when opaque, covered, and non-explicit.
- Belly dancer styling is allowed when presented as dance/fashion/cultural fantasy, not explicit content.
- No nudity.
- No sheer fabric.
- No visible underwear.
- No exposed intimate areas.
- No explicit sexual pose or act.
- No explicit emphasis on isolated body parts.
- No minor-coded styling, schoolgirl framing, or infantilized sexual styling.

## Angle And Pose Matrix

Use varied camera angles to study how face, figure, and silhouette appear from different directions.

Good angles:

- Full-body front portrait.
- Three-quarter front view.
- Side profile while standing.
- Three-quarter back view with hair visible.
- Seated side view on bed or chair.
- Standing near a table or counter.
- Walking frame in a private room.
- Looking down while reading or using a laptop.
- Adjusting hair clip.
- Holding a mug, book, phone, or laptop.

Pose should feel natural and candid. Avoid stiff model posing.

## Do And Don't Rules

Do:

- Preserve identity cues and body proportions.
- Celebrate curves through silhouette, fabric, and movement.
- Use varied angles and creative outfit categories.
- Keep the model clearly adult and realistically proportioned.
- Use sensuality through mood, confidence, styling, and composition.
- Keep lighting, pose, and setting believable.
- Keep skin texture, hair texture, and fabric behavior realistic.

Don't:

- Do not generate nudity or exposed intimate areas.
- Do not generate explicit sexual acts or explicit sexual posing.
- Do not use transparent/sheer fabric to reveal intimate areas.
- Do not isolate or fetishize body parts.
- Do not make the subject look underage, schoolgirl-coded, or infantilized.
- Do not exaggerate anatomy beyond the reference body model.
- Do not erase the natural softness of the face or body.
- Do not force every image into the same cozy sweater-dress look.

- Face still resembles the reference model.
- Hair length and texture remain consistent.
- Body structure is not over-slimmed or exaggerated.
- Outfit is tasteful, adult, and non-explicit for its category.
- Pose is natural and candid.
- Setting feels believable and intentional.
- Image celebrates figure through silhouette, movement, fabric, and pose, not explicit exposure.
- No explicit content, no text, no watermark.
