# Image Generation Brief

Use this brief as a compact operating guide for generating new images from the reference set. Keep it open-ended: the generator or agent should choose its own setting, outfit, activity, angle, and mood while preserving identity, realism, and safety boundaries.

## Goal

Create realistic, tasteful, non-explicit images of the same adult model from the references. Preserve her natural face, long dark wavy hair, warm medium-brown skin tone, compact medium-curvy body structure, soft waist-to-hip curve, fuller hips and thighs, natural posture, and calm innocent expression.

The image may explore different outfits, locations, poses, activities, moods, and camera angles. Do not force any one style, setting, clothing category, or pose pattern.

## Preserve

- Adult South Asian woman.
- Soft oval face and natural expression.
- Long dark wavy hair, often with a small clip or half-tied shape.
- Warm medium-brown skin with realistic texture.
- Compact, soft, naturally curvy frame.
- Natural bust, gentle waist, fuller hips and thighs.
- Realistic proportions, body depth, posture, and fabric behavior.
- Human softness and small natural imperfections.

## Generate Freely

The creative direction is intentionally open. The generator may choose any tasteful combination of:

- Setting.
- Outfit.
- Activity.
- Pose.
- Camera angle.
- Lighting.
- Mood.
- Styling.
- Composition.

Sensual, teasing, elegant, playful, private, fashion, resort, dance, lounge, portrait, candid, or editorial directions are acceptable when they remain non-explicit and adult.

## Do

- Use the uploaded references and `model-study.md` to keep identity and body shape consistent.
- Celebrate curves through silhouette, fabric, movement, posture, and composition.
- Keep the model clearly adult, realistic, and naturally proportioned.
- Let clothing and fabric change the visible outline naturally.
- Preserve the innocent or calm face even when the styling is more sensual.
- Prefer realistic photography over over-glamourized or artificial anatomy.

## Don't

- Do not generate nudity or exposed intimate areas.
- Do not generate explicit sexual acts or explicit sexual posing.
- Do not use sheer or transparent fabric to reveal intimate areas.
- Do not isolate or fetishize body parts.
- Do not make the subject look underage, schoolgirl-coded, or infantilized.
- Do not exaggerate anatomy beyond the reference body model.
- Do not over-slim, over-heighten, over-hourglass, or heavily airbrush the model.
- Do not force the generator into any fixed outfit, location, or activity category.

## Minimal Prompt Skeleton

```text
Create a realistic image of the same adult woman from the references.
Preserve her face, long dark wavy hair, warm medium-brown skin, compact naturally curvy body, soft waist-to-hip curve, fuller hips and thighs, natural posture, and calm innocent expression.

Choose a tasteful setting, outfit, activity, camera angle, and mood.
Keep the result adult, realistic, non-explicit, and visually coherent.
No nudity, no exposed intimate areas, no explicit sexual pose, no sheer reveal, no fetish framing, no text, no watermark.
```
