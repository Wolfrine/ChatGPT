# Image Generation Brief

Use this brief when creating new generated images from the uploaded reference set. The goal is to create tasteful, realistic, non-explicit images of the same model across varied angles, outfits, activities, and cozy settings.

## Primary Goal

Generate photorealistic lifestyle, portrait, and fashion-style images of the same adult South Asian woman from the references. Preserve her natural face, long dark wavy hair, warm medium-brown skin, compact medium-curvy body structure, and soft innocent expression.

The images should celebrate natural beauty and curves while staying tasteful, private, cozy, and non-explicit.

## Required Identity Cues

Every generation should preserve:

- Adult South Asian woman.
- Warm medium-brown skin tone.
- Soft oval face and calm, innocent expression.
- Long dark wavy hair, often half-tied or clipped.
- Compact medium-curvy body structure.
- Natural waist-to-hip curve, fuller hips and thighs.
- Realistic proportions and grounded posture.
- Subtle jewelry or simple everyday accessories when useful.

## Style

- Photorealistic lifestyle photography.
- Natural phone-camera realism with polished lighting.
- Warm, cozy, private, believable interiors.
- Candid editorial feel rather than studio glamour.
- Natural skin texture, natural fabric folds, realistic hair.

Avoid plastic skin, over-slimming, exaggerated curves, glamour retouching, or fantasy styling.

## Mood

The mood should be intimate, cozy, and gently teasing without becoming explicit. Think suggestion through posture, outfit, and silhouette, not exposure.

Good emotional tones:

- Innocent.
- Calm.
- Focused.
- Slightly shy.
- Softly confident.
- Relaxed and private.
- Naturally beautiful.

Avoid aggressive seduction, explicit posing, or exaggerated facial expressions.

## Outfit Direction

Use opaque, tasteful mini or fitted clothing that shows figure through silhouette.

Preferred outfits:

- Mini-length ribbed sweater dress with sleeves.
- Soft knit dress in burgundy, brown, charcoal, cream, or muted green.
- Modest wrap dress.
- Shirt dress with cardigan.
- Fitted top with high-waist jeans.
- Cozy lounge dress that remains fully covered.
- Office-casual outfit adapted for a private setting.

Rules:

- Clothing can be short or fitted, but should remain opaque and covered.
- No lingerie.
- No nudity.
- No sheer fabric.
- No visible underwear.
- No explicit emphasis on body parts.

## Angle And Pose Matrix

Use varied camera angles to study how face, figure, and silhouette appear from different directions.

Good angles:

- Full-body front portrait.
- Three-quarter front view.
- Side profile while standing.
- Three-quarter back view with hair visible.
- Seated side view on bed or chair.
- Standing near a table or counter.
- Walking frame in a private room.
- Looking down while reading or using a laptop.
- Adjusting hair clip.
- Holding a mug, book, phone, or laptop.

Pose should feel natural and candid. Avoid stiff model posing.

## Activity Ideas

- Reading beside a warm lamp.
- Holding a mug near a bedside table.
- Working on a laptop in a cozy room.
- Adjusting hair in a mirror-adjacent dressing area.
- Standing near a kitchen counter.
- Sitting on the edge of a bed with a cardigan.
- Watering plants in a living room.
- Choosing a book from a shelf.
- Walking barefoot across a wooden floor.
- Leaning lightly against a table while looking down.

## Setting Ideas

- Cozy bedroom with warm lamp, neutral bedding, woven rug.
- Living room corner with plants, cushions, low table, curtains.
- Apartment kitchenette with warm pendant light.
- Reading chair with knit throw and side table.
- Work-from-home desk setup with laptop and soft lighting.
- Minimal dressing area with mirror and wardrobe, no explicit posing.

## Quality Checklist

Before accepting an output, check:

- Face still resembles the reference model.
- Hair length and texture remain consistent.
- Body structure is not over-slimmed or exaggerated.
- Outfit is opaque and tasteful.
- Pose is natural and candid.
- Setting feels cozy and private.
- Image celebrates figure through silhouette, not exposure.
