# Model Study

This document summarizes the visual traits to preserve when generating new images from the uploaded reference set. The goal is a tasteful, non-explicit portrait and fashion-style study that celebrates natural beauty, face, figure, posture, and presence across different angles and activities.

## Core Impression

The model has a soft, natural, understated beauty. The strongest visual quality is the contrast between an innocent, calm face and a naturally curvy, feminine silhouette. The references work best when they feel candid rather than staged: office moments, laptop work, walking, sitting, reading, and quiet private scenes.

The overall look should stay grounded and real. Avoid overly glamourized, airbrushed, or exaggerated outputs. Preserve normal human posture, real fabric behavior, natural hair texture, and small everyday imperfections.

## Face And Expression

- Adult South Asian woman with warm medium-brown skin.
- Soft oval face with a gentle, composed expression.
- Innocent, quiet, focused presence rather than overt seduction.
- Natural eyes and brows, subtle makeup or no-makeup styling.
- Expressions should be calm, thoughtful, shy, focused, or softly smiling.
- Avoid exaggerated seductive faces, heavy glamour makeup, or dramatic model poses.

## Hair

- Long, dark, thick, wavy hair is a major identity cue.
- Often half-tied or clipped back with a small hair clip.
- Hair should have volume, natural texture, and slight frizz or flyaways.
- Back and side angles should show the length and wave pattern clearly.
- Avoid over-smoothed salon hair or unrealistic glossy curls.

## Body And Figure

- Compact, medium-curvy body structure.
- Soft natural waist-to-hip curve.
- Fuller hips and thighs are a key silhouette feature.
- Bust is natural and should be represented through clothing shape, not exposure.
- Arms and shoulders are soft and proportionate.
- Body should feel real and grounded, not stretched, slimmed, or exaggerated.

The most flattering visual emphasis comes from silhouette, fabric fit, and posture: side profile, three-quarter back, seated side view, standing with crossed legs, leaning at a table, adjusting hair, or holding a laptop/book/mug.

## Best Angles

- Three-quarter front: shows face, hair, waist, and outfit shape together.
- Side profile: highlights natural posture and body curve.
- Three-quarter back: emphasizes hair length and hip silhouette without being explicit.
- Seated side angle: works well for casual activities and curve study.
- Full-body vertical framing: best for outfit and figure consistency.
- Slight candid phone-camera angles can work if they remain tasteful and realistic.

## Clothing Observations

The references show that fitted but opaque clothing works best. The outfit should suggest shape through fabric and silhouette rather than exposure.

Strong outfit directions:

- Ribbed sweater dresses.
- Mini-length knit dresses with sleeves.
- Wrap dresses with tasteful coverage.
- Shirt dresses with cardigan layers.
- Fitted tops with jeans or high-waist trousers.
- Soft lounge-inspired but public-safe clothing.
- Office-casual outfits that show natural figure through fit.

Avoid lingerie, sheer fabric, nudity, visible underwear, or poses that turn the image into explicit content.

## Setting Notes

The original references have a candid office quality: laptops, desks, meeting rooms, chairs, glass walls, cables, and soft indoor light. New generations can expand into private cozy settings while keeping the same believable, candid feel.

Good settings:

- Cozy bedroom reading corner.
- Warm living room with floor lamp and plants.
- Apartment kitchenette or dining nook.
- Desk or work-from-home setup.
- Mirror-adjacent dressing area without explicit posing.
- Soft morning/evening private interior.

## Visual Guardrails

- Celebrate curves through silhouette, angle, and clothing fit.
- Keep the mood intimate but wholesome.
- Maintain adult, realistic proportions.
- Preserve identity cues: face, hair, skin tone, body structure, and calm presence.
- Keep all outputs non-explicit: no nudity, no lingerie focus, no fetish framing, no transparent fabric.
- Do not over-sexualize the face or pose.
