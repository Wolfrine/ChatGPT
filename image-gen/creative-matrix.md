# Creative Matrix

Use this matrix to keep new generations varied. Agents should combine entries across columns rather than repeating the same office, balcony, mirror, or bedroom concepts already present in `created/`.

## Already Common In `created/`

The existing generated set heavily uses:

- Office and laptop environments.
- Bedroom or warm lamp reading scenes.
- Mirror/getting-ready scenes.
- Mini dress or mini skirt office styling.
- Balcony/terrace/resort/poolside variants.
- Side profile, three-quarter rear, low-angle doorway, and seated office views.

These can still be used, but only with a clearly new twist in outfit, environment, activity, camera angle, and mood.

## Underused Environment Ideas

- Boutique fitting room with soft curtain panels.
- Hotel-suite kitchenette at night with city lights.
- Private library nook with tall shelves and a floor lamp.
- Rainy window alcove with reflections on glass.
- Candlelit dining corner after an event.
- Walk-in closet choosing shoes or jewelry.
- Spa changing area with a plush robe over opaque loungewear.
- Rooftop garden at blue hour.
- Art studio with canvas, fabric drape, and warm side light.
- Music room with vinyl records or a keyboard.
- Quiet hallway outside an elevator with cinematic light.
- Sunlit bathroom vanity, non-revealing and editorial.
- Resort cabana with linen curtains and non-sheer cover-up.
- Courtyard staircase with evening dress movement.
- Home office after-hours with blazer, camisole-style opaque top, and trousers.

## Outfit Rotation

Keep clothing opaque and non-explicit. Vary fabric behavior:

- Satin midi wrap dress.
- Ribbed knit dress with cardigan.
- High-waisted trousers with fitted blouse.
- Tailored blazer over opaque camisole-style top.
- Denim skirt with soft knit top.
- Saree with modern blouse, tasteful drape, no excessive exposure.
- Long slip-style dress under an open cardigan or shrug.
- Linen resort co-ord or non-sheer cover-up.
- Evening gown with side slit kept tasteful.
- Velvet dress or velvet blazer set.
- Soft lounge set with robe, opaque and elegant.
- Pencil skirt with tucked blouse.
- Pleated skirt and fitted sweater, not school-coded.
- Wide-leg pants with cropped-but-opaque jacket/top.
- Traditional kurta or anarkali with cinched dupatta movement.

## Angle And Composition Rotation

- Eye-level candid three-quarter front.
- Seated side view at a vanity or window.
- High-angle full-body lifestyle shot.
- Low but tasteful fashion angle that does not fetishize.
- Over-the-shoulder mirror shot showing face in reflection.
- Walking mid-step with motion in hair and fabric.
- Leaning at a table with hands visible and natural.
- Doorway silhouette with full-scene context.
- Profile portrait with body depth preserved.
- Three-quarter back view where face is partly visible.
- Close portrait from waist-up, not body-part focused.
- Full-body vertical editorial crop.
- Environmental wide shot with the subject smaller in frame.

## Activity And Gesture Rotation

- Adjusting earrings, bracelet, watch, or hair clip.
- Reading a book or holding a coffee cup.
- Choosing a dress or shoes from a closet.
- Looking out a rainy window.
- Walking across a balcony or hallway.
- Sitting on the edge of a sofa or window bench.
- Turning back toward camera naturally.
- Draping a shawl, dupatta, cardigan, or robe.
- Picking up a clutch before going out.
- Writing in a notebook.
- Touching a curtain or opening balcony doors.
- Standing near artwork or plants.

## Lighting And Mood Rotation

- Warm tungsten lamp light.
- Blue-hour city-window light.
- Soft rainy daylight.
- Candlelit ambient glow.
- Golden-hour backlight.
- Poolside reflected sunlight.
- Moody low-key studio side light.
- Soft dressing-room bulbs.
- Bright candid phone-photo office light.
- Natural overcast outdoor light.

## Concept Selection Rule

Before generating, write a one-line concept and compare it to `agent-ledger.md`. If a concept matches an existing entry on more than two of these axes, revise it:

1. Environment.
2. Outfit.
3. Camera angle/crop.
4. Activity/gesture.
5. Lighting/mood.
